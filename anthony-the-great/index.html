Full Biography

St. Anthony (c. 251–356) is known as the "Father of All Monks." Born in Egypt, he heard the Gospel command to "sell all you have and give to the poor," which led him into the deep desert to seek God in solitude.

Life Timeline

251 AD

Born in Coma, Lower Egypt to wealthy landowners.

270 AD

Hears the Gospel, distributes his wealth, and begins ascetic life.

285 AD

Moves to the "Outer Mountain" at Pispir to live in total isolation.

356 AD

Departs this life at age 105, buried in a secret location.

Sayings (Apophthegmata)

"I saw the snares that the enemy spreads out over the world and I said groaning, 'What can get through from such snares?' Then I heard a voice saying to me, 'Humility.'"
"Our life and our death is with our neighbor. If we gain our brother, we have gained God."
